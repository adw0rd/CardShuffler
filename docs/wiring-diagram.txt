═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                                   NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. LX-LCBST — boost converter steps up 3.7V to 5V to power the entire circuit

2. TB6612FNG VM — can be connected to 5V or a separate source up to 13.5V
   for more powerful motors

3. Motor direction — fixed via AIN1/BIN1=HIGH, AIN2/BIN2=LOW

4. ST7735 — powered from 3.3V (from RP2040-Zero onboard regulator)

5. Encoder — with built-in pull-up resistors; if not present,
   add 10kΩ pull-ups to 3V3
