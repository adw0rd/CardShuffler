═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
                                   NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. LX-LCBST — boost converter steps up 3.7V to 5V to power the entire circuit

2. TB6612FNG VCC+VM — powered directly from LX-LCBST 5V (not via RP2040)
   to handle motor current (FA-130 up to ~800mA each)

3. Motor direction — fixed via AIN1/BIN1=HIGH, AIN2/BIN2=LOW

4. Motors — 2x FA-130 (∅20mm), nominal 1.5–3V, max ~4.5V
   PWM limited to 65% via MOTOR_MAX_SPEED in config.h (~3.25V effective at 5V VM)

5. ST7735 — powered from 3.3V (from RP2040-Zero onboard regulator)

6. Encoder — internal pull-ups enabled in firmware (gpio_pull_up),
   no external resistors needed
